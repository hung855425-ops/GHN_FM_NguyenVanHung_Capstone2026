Real Case Analysis

Pain Point

Team Leaders/Operations Staff haven't paid enough attention to employees' income & well-being.

Impact

  • Employees quit due to low pay (job postings advertise 12M VND but actual pay is lower).
  • Employees repeatedly work overtime (checking out later than 6 PM).
  • Team Leaders fail to catch this in time.

Root Cause

Team Leaders are focused on goods operations and don't have time to manually analyze HR data.

Proposed Solution

Use n8n + AI for automatic tracking:

  • Filter employees with salary below average.
  • Filter employees checking out after 6 PM.
  • Daily reports sent via Telegram.

Expected Results

100% automation of tracking.

Increased Retention Rate Increased GTC % Increased Order Volume
